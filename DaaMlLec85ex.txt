2. Regression Formula
# y = mx+c

Ye Linear Regression ka formula hai.

y → output, yani Salary
x → input, yani Experience
m → coefficient/slope
c → intercept

Real life: Experience badhne par salary kitni badh rahi hai, ye formula calculate karta hai.

3. m aur c set karna
m = 5
c = 20
m = 5 → har 1 year experience par salary 5 units badh rahi hai.
c = 20 → starting/base value.
4. Input value
x = 9

Matlab employee ka experience 9 years hai.

5. Prediction
m*x+c

Formula:

5 × 9 + 20 = 65

So output:

65

Yani 9 years experience → predicted salary 65.
